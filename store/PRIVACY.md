# Privacy policy for Hubble Nebulae Wallpapers

Hubble Nebulae Wallpapers is an independent open-source desktop app published by jldevstudio. The app does not require an account and does not contain advertising, analytics or telemetry. The publisher does not receive the app's downloaded images, wallpaper settings or sync history.

The app connects directly to NASA Science to retrieve public gallery pages and image files when you sync. After the first sync, it periodically checks for new images while it is running. NASA's servers receive ordinary web request data such as your IP address. NASA describes its handling of web requests in its [privacy policy](https://www.nasa.gov/privacy/).

Images and the last sync time are stored on your device. If you set the wallpaper slideshow, the app asks Windows to use the downloaded image folder. You can stop network checks by choosing Exit in the notification area or by uninstalling the app.

For privacy questions, contact the publisher through the [project repository](https://github.com/jldevstudio/hubble-nebulae-wallpapers).
